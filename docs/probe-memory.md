# Incentive probe: he remembers you

150 seeded **simulated** players per strategy play three Heists (Bank 3) in a row, the same body every job. With memory, every safe of a job (the same numbers his model learns from) is carried into the next job: the last job counts 0.7, each job before that 0.7 times less, and he keeps the newest 40 safes. Without memory, every job starts from his priors. Mean pile per job; the paired column compares the third job with honest play.
Generated by `tests/engine/memory-probe.test.ts` from the simulated body in `src/renderer/engine/sim.ts`. Not recorded people.
