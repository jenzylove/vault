## Rules in force

- Heat: +0.34 per trap the banker buys and finds at its audit, +0.1 per unread peek, up to +0.15 from a rising pulse. At 1 the banker throws you out with 0% of your pile.
- Heist: a trap you sold pays its price again (×1) when the banker's audit finds it.
- Heat from pulse counts only for banks that read pulse (4 and 5).
- The banker never buys a box where it saw you catch your breath. A box it lost you at that turns out to be a trap counts as caught.
- Banker walks when P(trap) ≥ its level's walk point − 0.2 × heat (floor 0.3). Offer = its estimate of a treasure's value × (1 − margin), margin = level base + 0.05 × heat + 0.05 × (1 − banker's own hit rate).
- Unread peeks get a blind offer at 50%, priced from what this player's unread boxes turned out to be.
- Banker model: per player Gaussian naive Bayes on breath catch, shallowing, rate change, blinks, smile, alarm; priors with 6 pseudo counts per class for breath and 2 for the rest, updated by 2 labelled practice peeks and every audit (each labelled box weighted by the level's learning weight). Signals a level can't read contribute nothing.

## Simulated player policy

- Sells a treasure only when the offer beats its value; opens it otherwise.
- Sells a trap whenever offered, unless that sale would leave too little heat budget to peek the rest of the board (rational); the greedy row sells every trap and never leaves.
