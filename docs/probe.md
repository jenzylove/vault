## Rules in force

- Strikes: a dud the banker buys and finds at its audit is 1 strike. At 2 strikes you are busted and leave with 0% of your pile. Nothing else busts you.
- Heat is his suspicion only: +0.5 per dud he found, +0.2 per unread peek, up to +0.15 from a rising pulse. It lowers his offers and makes him quicker to pass.
- Ticking offer: his offer falls from 100% to 60% over 5 s; the simulated players answer at once, so they get the full offer.
- Last safe: with one safe left and a take on the line you may walk out or bet the take double or nothing on his call. The simulated players walk out (the default policy).
- Heist: a trap you sold pays its price again (×1) when the banker's audit finds it (not on a blind offer).
- Heat from pulse counts only for banks that read pulse (4 and 5).
- The banker never buys a box where it saw you catch your breath. A box it lost you at that turns out to be a trap counts as caught.
- Banker walks when P(trap) ≥ its level's walk point − 0.2 × heat (floor 0.3). Offer = its estimate of a treasure's value × (1 − margin), margin = level base + 0.05 × heat + 0.05 × (1 − banker's own hit rate).
- Unread peeks get a blind offer at 50%, priced from what this player's unread boxes turned out to be.
- Banker model: per player Gaussian naive Bayes on breath catch, shallowing, rate change, blinks, smile, alarm; priors with 6 pseudo counts per class for breath and 2 for the rest, updated by every audit (and by 2 labelled practice peeks when the player takes them; the probe skips them, as most runs do) (each labelled box weighted by the level's learning weight). Signals a level can't read contribute nothing.

## Simulated player policy

- A run is up to 5 peeks out of 10 boxes, picked at random; 10 s calm, 5 s peeks, no practice; it walks out at the last safe when it has a take.
- Sells a treasure only when the offer beats its value; opens it otherwise.
- Sells a dud whenever offered, unless the strike would bust it (rational); the greedy row sells every dud.
