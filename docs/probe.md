## Rules in force

- Heat: +0.3 per trap the banker buys and finds at its audit, +0.1 per unread peek, up to +0.15 from a rising pulse. At 1 the banker throws you out with 50% of your pile.
- Banker walks when P(trap) ≥ 0.6 − 0.2 × heat (floor 0.3). Offer = belief value × (1 − margin), margin = 0.2 + 0.2 × heat + 0.15 × (1 − banker's own hit rate).
- Unread peeks get a blind offer at 50%, priced from what this player's unread boxes turned out to be.
- Banker model: per player Gaussian naive Bayes on breath catch, shallowing, rate change, blinks, smile, alarm; priors with 2 pseudo counts per class, updated by 2 labelled practice peeks and every audit.

## Simulated player policy

- Sells a treasure only when the offer beats its value; opens it otherwise.
- Sells a trap whenever offered, unless that sale would leave too little heat budget to peek the rest of the board (rational); the greedy row sells every trap and never leaves.
